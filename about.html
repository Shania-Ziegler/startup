About the sanctuary

Quantum hardware, learned by caring for it.

Quantum computers can seem difficult to approach because their hardware is often introduced through equations first. Quantum Sanctuary takes a different route.

You are an apprentice restoring a sanctuary of quantum spirits. Each chamber represents a different kind of quantum-computing hardware. Instead of moving the spirits directly, you experiment with the controls available to that physical system and watch how it responds.

In the Quiet Chamber, Lumi is a trapped ion. Laser pulses change her internal state and motion. By experimenting with pulse sequences, you gradually discover what those controls mean.
