    About

    Learn how quantum computer hardware works by solving puzzles with quantum particles.

    Each chamber holds a different kind of quantum hardware: trapped ions, neutral atoms, photons, superconducting circuits, and silicon spin qubits. You build a short experiment, run it, and see how the particle responds. Chambers can be shared, so you can solve them with friends.
